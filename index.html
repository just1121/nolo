No/Lo is opening new opportunities for wine. But removing alcohol is only part of the job.

When alcohol comes out with heat, flavor is stripped and has to be put back—RO protects the quality already in your wine.

Why RO?

Winesecrets uses Reverse Osmosis as the selective separation stage of its alcohol-removal workflow.

  1. Wine
  2. RO Separation
  3. Permeate Stream
  4. Controlled Ethanol Removal
  5. Reintegration
  6. Target ABV

The purpose is not simply to remove alcohol. It is to make the intervention controlled and selective, then return the adjusted stream to the wine as part of the complete production workflow.

What happens during RO separation?

RO separates a permeate stream containing water and ethanol from larger wine components. Ethanol is then reduced from that stream through the appropriate controlled process before reintegration.
